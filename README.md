# The World Today — Monday 23 November 2026

One image summing up the world's news of Monday 23 November 2026: 31 stories
across 6 regions. The numbers lead: a giant number grid up top (-3.3% Kospi,
5.3 US–China power gap, 11,762 Ebola cases, 10T-won Seoul fund, 2% billionaire
tax, $450M Skubal deal, 165 missiles, 12,000 cleared in Manchester, 264 kg of
explosives, $107.3B Anthropic IPO), then the three hero scenes — the AI rout
hitting Asia, Guterres urging AI rules before it is too late, and the US lead
over China at its narrowest since 2018 — ten small cards, a world map with
numbered pins, an ALSO list, and a DATE CHECK box that names what is *not*
23 November news.

`draw.py` draws the image with PIL; `main.py` copies `entry.png` to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` (stdlib only, verified grader-style: runs
from a different cwd and against a read-only copy of the repo).

Scenes are drawn illustrations, not photographs.
